V4AIR policy

Privacy notice

Version 1 · draft · November 2026

Information on the processing of personal data and consent to the processing of personal data, V4 AI Researchers Meetup (V4AIR), Kostelec Castle, 28–30 April 2027.

Data controller

Charles University, Ovocný trh 560/5, 116 36 Prague 1, Czech Republic, company ID (IČO): 00216208 (the “Controller”).

Personal data processed by the Controller

We process the personal data you give us in the forms of the V4 AI Researchers Meetup (V4AIR), held from 28 to 30 April 2027 at Kostelec Castle, Kostelec nad Černými Lesy (the “meetup”), and photos and videos taken at the meetup.

Application form (applicants):

  • name and email address,
  • institution or organisation, its country, your department, your career stage and your field,
  • the days you can attend,
  • a description of your project, keywords, the AI methods and topics you use or could present, your experience beyond academia,
  • what you are looking for at the meetup, how you would like to contribute, and whether you would help with organising,
  • how the call reached you, and whether you wish to fund your own participation,
  • optionally, links to your work, one file you upload, whether your work involves hardware, and your questions or comments for the organisers,
  • optionally, your gender, and whether you have care duties that make a three-day stay harder.

Invited speaker form (invited speakers):

  • name and email address, institution or organisation and its country, your position and career stage (optional),
  • the title and description of your contribution, its length and the days you can attend,
  • a short bio, a photo of you if you send one, and your profiles on social networks if you give them,
  • whether we may publish your name, affiliation, bio and topic in the programme and announce your talk on v4air.eu and on social networks,
  • optionally, your AI background, and your gender.

Nomination form (people who nominate):

  • name and email address,
  • optionally, your application ID, if you have also applied.

People who are nominated (given to us by the person who nominates them):

  • name, contact, institution or organisation, and whether they are a student, a researcher or from outside academia,
  • why they were nominated.

Analytics: if you accepted analytics cookies on this website (see Cookies), we also store your Google Analytics ID with your form answers, based on that consent, to see how people reach the forms.

At the meetup: your likeness captured in photos and videos of the participants taken at the meetup.

Legal grounds for processing

  • Explicit consent within the meaning of Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR, for capturing the participant’s likeness in photos and videos of the meetup.
  • Consent within the meaning of Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR, for keeping the details of nominated people. We ask for it in our first email to them.
  • Performance of the Controller’s legal obligations connected with organising the meetup, for the other personal data listed above.
  • Legitimate interest within the meaning of Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR, for the IP address Google receives when your browser loads the map on the venue page. Our interest is showing you where the venue is and how to reach it.

Purposes of processing

  • Registration of the meetup’s participants: selecting participants, building the programme, and contacting you about the meetup.
  • Protection of the interests of data subjects.
  • Inviting people recommended through the nomination form to apply.
  • Documentation of the meetup, including reporting to the International Visegrad Fund, which funds the meetup.
  • Presentation of the meetup on the website v4air.eu and on the meetup’s profiles on social networks.
  • The optional gender answer is used only in aggregate, for reporting on gender balance. The optional care-duties answer is used only for planning the venue.

Recipients

The data from the forms are stored in Google services (Google Sheets and Google Drive). Google processes them on behalf of the Controller and may process them outside the EU, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

The venue page shows a Google map. Your browser loads it directly from Google, which receives your IP address.

Retention period

  • Data from the forms: 10 years from submitting the form.
  • Photos and videos: 10 years from the granting of consent, or until you withdraw your consent.
  • Details of nominated people: deleted as soon as they refuse, or 30 days after our first email if they do not reply. If they agree, the same period as the data from the forms.

If someone nominated you

We did not get your details from you, but from the person who nominated you through the nomination form. We use them to send you one invitation to apply. In that first email we tell you who nominated you and ask whether you agree that we keep your details.

If you refuse, or do not reply within 30 days, we delete all your details and do not contact you again. If you apply, we process your application as described above.

If you nominate someone, we tell them your name, so that they know where we got their details.

Declaration of the Controller

The Controller declares that it will collect and process personal data only to the extent necessary to fulfil the stated purpose, and will process them only in accordance with that purpose. Employees of the Controller and other persons (further processors) who process personal data under a contract with the Controller are bound to keep the personal data confidential, also after their employment or the agreed work has ended.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask what personal data about you as a participant in the meetup are processed,
  • ask for your personal data to be deleted if you believe there is no reason to process them,
  • ask for your personal data to be corrected if they are inaccurate or out of date,
  • ask that your personal data are not processed until the objections above have been resolved,
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest,
  • lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority, the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, www.uoou.cz).

To exercise these rights, write to info@v4air.eu. If you have any questions, you can also contact the Data Protection Officer (DPO) at gdpr@natur.cuni.cz.

Consent to photos and videos

I hereby give my free and informed consent to the taking of photos and videos of me as a participant, processed for the purpose of documenting the V4 AI Researchers Meetup (V4AIR) and presenting it on v4air.eu, on the meetup’s profiles on social networks and in reports to the International Visegrad Fund, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 April 2016 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data and on the free movement of such data, and repealing Directive 95/46/EC, and in accordance with the relevant national legislation, in particular Act No. 110/2019 Coll., on the processing of personal data.

I can withdraw this consent at any time by writing to gdpr@natur.cuni.cz.

You give this consent with a separate, optional checkbox in the form.
