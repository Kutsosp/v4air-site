V4AIR policy

Code of Conduct

Version 1 · draft · November 2026

Purpose

V4AIR brings together people from different fields, countries and career stages. We want everyone to be able to take part fully, speak freely and feel safe for all three days.

Our values

We treat each other with respect and good faith. We welcome everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, disability, ethnicity, nationality, religion or belief, appearance, career stage or field.

Where the code applies

The code applies to everyone at V4AIR: participants, speakers, invited experts, organisers and guests. It applies in every part of the meetup:

  • talks and workshops,
  • meals and evening social events,
  • the accommodation,
  • online spaces connected to the meetup.

We live and work together at the castle for three days, so the code covers the whole stay, not only the programme.

Unacceptable behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable at V4AIR:

  • offensive or demeaning remarks about a person’s personal characteristics,
  • intimidation, threats, stalking or unwanted following,
  • unwelcome physical contact, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances,
  • repeated disruption of talks or discussions,
  • using seniority, or the prospect of a job or collaboration, to pressure or silence someone.

Encouraging any of this behaviour is also not acceptable.

Presenters

Talks, demos, slides and other materials follow the same standard. They must not contain sexualised or demeaning content.

If something happens

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this code, tell one of our contact persons. You can speak to them in person or write to them by email at any time during the meetup. We will name the contact persons here before the meetup.

We handle every report discreetly. We share information only as far as we need to act. Nobody will be penalised for reporting in good faith.

In an emergency, call 112, the European emergency number.

What we may do

Organisers may take any action they consider appropriate, from a conversation or a warning to exclusion from parts of the meetup or from the whole meetup.

A person excluded from the whole meetup leaves the venue, including the accommodation, as soon as this can be done safely. We do not cover any extra costs this causes, such as an earlier journey home.

Where needed, we will help contact the venue staff or the police.

Agreement

By taking part in V4AIR, everyone agrees to follow this code.
