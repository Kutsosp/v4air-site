Prices

V4 AI Researchers Meetup, Kostelec Castle, 28-30 April 2027

Participation fee

The participation fee is one price for the whole meetup.

ParticipantParticipation fee
Student120 €
Professional160 €

What the participation fee includes

  • Full access to the three-day program
  • Two breakfasts (29 and 30 April)
  • Two lunches (29 and 30 April)
  • Dinner and opening reception (28 April), including alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks
  • Five coffee breaks with coffee and tea
  • Your badge and program

Need help with the costs?

Don't let the price stop you from applying. Write to info@v4air.eu and we will do our best to cover your expenses.

Gala dinner

On 29 April, participants can attend a gala dinner at the National Museum of Brewing, with beer tasting and a guided tour.

The price of the gala dinner will be announced soon.

Accommodation

The price covers two nights, from Wednesday 28 April to Friday 30 April, and includes the local accommodation tax. The number of rooms of each type is limited.

Other expenses

You pay for your own travel to and from Kostelec Castle.

If you have a question about prices, write to us at info@v4air.eu.
