The venue · 28-30 April 2027

Three days in a Renaissance castle near Prague

V4AIR takes place at Kostelec Castle, 45 minutes from Prague. Your room, your meals and every session are a few minutes’ walk apart, and the forest begins at the edge of town.

Apply nowSee the castle

The castle

Seven centuries old, and still in daily use

A royal castle stood here by the 13th century. After a fire in 1548 it was rebuilt as the Renaissance castle you see today. In 1618 its young owner, Albrecht Jan Smiřický, was spoken of as a candidate for the Bohemian throne.

Today the castle belongs to the Czech University of Life Sciences Prague, which also manages the forests around the town. It is a working university building, not a museum.

Where you sleep

Your room is in the castle or next to it

Participants stay in the castle and in the historic guesthouses around it, all within a few minutes’ walk of the courtyard.

Meals, sessions and evenings happen on the same few streets. You will not need a car or a taxi during the three days.

Down the street

A working brewery, six minutes from the castle

Kostelec has brewed beer since 1489. The town brewery, a short walk from the castle gate, has brewed again since 2009. Its courtyard has a pub, long tables outside and a brewing museum.

Look for the eight-sided hall where horses once walked in circles to drive the machinery. It is probably the largest building of its kind in Europe.

The black forests

The town is named after its forests

Kostelec nad Černými lesy means “Kostelec above the black forests”. The woods begin at the edge of town.

Five kilometres away is Voděradské bučiny, a national nature reserve: 684 hectares of beech forest with granite boulders and forest springs. In late April the beeches are coming into leaf. Bring shoes you can walk in.

Getting there

By metro and bus from Prague

Kostelec is 35 km east of Prague. By public transport, take metro line C to Háje, then a direct bus to the town square, four minutes from the castle gate. From Prague airport, allow two to two and a half hours.

Access

The castle is not step-free

It is a historic building with stairs throughout and cobbled courtyards. If you use a wheelchair or find stairs difficult, please write to us at info@v4air.eu before you apply. We will look for a solution with the venue.

Telling us has no effect on selection.
