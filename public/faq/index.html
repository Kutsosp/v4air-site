Questions, answered

What does "fully funded" actually cover?

Everything essential: accommodation at the castle, board for the whole three days, and a contribution toward travel scaled by the distance of your institution. Funding comes from the International Visegrad Fund.

How many places are there?

Funded places go to selected participants from the six partner universities, plus a limited number of additional seats for a fee.

How are participants selected?

Based on your answers in the application form. We select people whose needs fit the event and who together make a good mix. There is no CV or motivation letter, and the order of applications does not matter.

I was nominated by my institution. Do I still apply?

Yes. Everyone fills in the same application form, nominated or not; the form simply asks who nominated you. A nomination gets you noticed, not selected.

Do I have to come for all three days?

In the form you tell us whether you can come for all three days, Wednesday midday to Friday evening (28-30 April 2027), and if not, what you know so far. The program is built from the selected participants, so you will know in good time which days matter most for you.

We hope you stay for all of it. Living, eating and talking together in one secluded castle is a big part of the experience, and many of the best conversations happen in the evenings.

What language is everything in?

English, throughout.

Where is Kostelec and how do I get there?

Kostelec nad Černými lesy is about 45 minutes from Prague. Fly into Prague (Václav Havel Airport), take the metro to Háje (line C), and a direct bus takes you to Kostelec. From the airport, allow two to two and a half hours. Accepted participants receive full travel instructions with their acceptance.

Is the castle accessible?

Kostelec is a Renaissance castle with stairs. If you have access needs of any kind, write to the organizers at info@v4air.eu before you apply and we will look for a solution together. This has no bearing on selection.

Who counts as "early-career"?

Master’s students, PhD candidates and postdocs. Researchers just past the postdoc stage, for example a new assistant professor starting a research group, are welcome too. If you are unsure, apply.

My university is not a partner. Can I still come?

Yes. A limited number of paid places are open to people from other institutions. The fees are on the prices page. Apply through the same form.

Do I need a paper, poster, or finished talk to apply?

No. Ideas and unfinished projects are welcome, and so are projects that failed or got stuck. If you are selected, we plan your part together.

Will my talk be recorded or published?

We plan to share talks, slides, and example code among participants after the meetup; you can opt out of having your material included. Details come with your acceptance.

What happens after the three days?

Follow-up activities for participants are being planned so that the connections made over the three days continue. Details come during the meetup.

I have an idea, I want to collaborate, or I have another question.

We want to hear it. Write to info@v4air.eu; ideas, collaboration offers, and questions all go straight to the organizing team.
