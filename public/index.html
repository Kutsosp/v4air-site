“But I’m not an AI researcher”

You don’t need to be. At V4AIR, “AI researchers” means researchers in any field who use AI in their research. Your questions come from your own field, and AI is one of the methods you use to answer them.

A historian working through thousands of archival documents belongs here as much as a computer scientist. So does a sports scientist using pose estimation to give young players feedback.

If you analyze qualitative data, such as interviews, texts or archives, large language models are a tool worth knowing. You don’t need to build them to use them well.

At the meetup you work with people who specialize in the methods you need, so you can bring the problem and leave with a way to approach it.

Not in academia? If you work with AI in a company, a startup, tech transfer or investment, apply through the same form, as a participant or as a speaker.

What you’ll take home

Ideas from other fields. You see how researchers in other fields use AI methods, and how those methods could work on your own problems.

Experts in the methods you need. You meet researchers from other fields who already use these methods and could work with you.

Real use cases for your methods. If you build or adapt AI methods, you meet researchers who bring real data and open questions from their fields: new applications, collaborations and papers.

The program

At a typical conference, you sit through talks that have little to do with your work.

At V4AIR, the program adapts to you instead. The talks and workshops are chosen to address your specific problem.

See how we fit the program to you →

What you can expect

  • Talks. Invited experts and participants talk about their work. The topics are chosen from what you write in your applications.

  • Workshops. Practical sessions on the methods participants ask for in the application form.

  • Not-Just-Posters session. For projects that don’t fit slides. Students show a poster, a prototype, a physical object or an interactive demo, and get feedback from experts.

  • Free time to talk. Everyone stays and eats within a few minutes’ walk of each other, so conversations continue after the sessions end.

  • Evenings at the castle. A welcome dinner on 28 April, a guided tour of the castle, and a gala dinner with beer tasting and a museum tour on 29 April at the National Museum of Brewing, the town brewery six minutes’ walk from the castle.

The venue

You will be staying in the castle

Kostelec Castle is a Renaissance castle owned by the Czech University of Life Sciences Prague, 45 minutes from Prague by metro and a direct bus. Rooms, meals and sessions are all within a few minutes’ walk of each other.

Working language: English.
