**Headline:** Three days at Kostelec Castle with the people your research needs

**Subject line (email):** V4AIR: meet the people your research needs, 28–30 April 2027

---

Do you use AI in your research? Then V4AIR is for you, whatever your field.

V4AIR, the V4 AI Researchers Meetup, brings students and early-career researchers from the four Visegrad countries to Kostelec Castle near Prague on 28–30 April 2027. For three days, you live and work in one place with people who apply AI across disciplines.

**Who it's for**

If AI is part of your research, you belong here. It can be the tool you use every day or the thing you study. A biologist training models on field data is as welcome as a PhD student building new architectures.

You can apply as a bachelor's or master's student, a PhD student, a postdoc, or a researcher still early in building a group. If you're not sure whether you count as early-career, apply.

**Meet the people your research needs**

When you apply, you tell us who you want to meet. We use your answers to put you in the room with them.

- Early-career researchers find collaborators and team members, get advice from experts, and talk to people from industry.
- Students find mentors and research positions, and get feedback on their ideas.

**You shape the programme**

The application form asks what you work on, what you want to learn and who you want to meet. From what you tell us, we choose the talks, the workshop topics and the invited speakers.

**You don't need a finished paper**

Bring the work you're doing now. Projects that got stuck are welcome too. Everyone contributes in some form, and if you're selected, we shape your contribution with you. Students who don't have results yet can present in the Not-Just-Posters session and get feedback on their project.

**Fully funded for the six partner universities**

If you study or work at a partner university, you pay no participation fee. The project covers:

- accommodation at the castle
- meals for the three days
- a contribution toward your travel, scaled by distance

The partner universities are Charles University, the Czech Technical University in Prague, Comenius University Bratislava, Eötvös Loránd University, the University of Wrocław, and Nicolaus Copernicus University in Toruń.

Students from other institutions can take part for 160 euro. If you're from another institution and not a student, write to us at kutsosp@natur.cuni.cz.

**How selection works**

There's no CV and no motivation letter. We select for fit and mix, so the order in which applications arrive doesn't matter. The core of your application is reviewed without your institution's name. A careful application is the best thing you can do for your chances.

**Practical details**

- **When:** Wednesday 28 April 2027 from midday to Friday 30 April in the evening. We expect you for all three days.
- **Where:** Kostelec Castle in Kostelec nad Černými lesy, about 30 minutes from Prague.
- **Language:** English.
- **Results:** February 2027.
