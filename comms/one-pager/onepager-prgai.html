prg.ai is co-organizing the V4 AI Researchers Meetup

In April 2027, PhD students and early-career researchers from Czechia, Hungary, Poland and Slovakia who use AI in their research meet for three days near Prague. We are inviting our partners and collaborators to join them.

What V4AIR is

Researchers in many fields use the same AI methods, yet they rarely meet. V4AIR matches them by method, across disciplines.
