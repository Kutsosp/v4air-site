V4 AI Researchers Meetup

Join the V4 AI Researchers Meetup

Co-organised by prg.ai and Charles University

In April 2027, PhD students, researchers and experts who apply AI methods will come from across Central Europe for three days at V4AIR near Prague. We invite our partners and collaborators to join them.

What do a bee researcher and a traffic engineer have in common?

They both use computer vision.
They are both coming to V4AIR.

Our goal: bring Central Europe's applied AI research and industry into the same room.

  • Kostelec Castle, 30 minutes from Prague
  • 28–30 April 2027
  • Three days, with accommodation at the castle

  • meeting a researcher with the expertise your problem needs
  • sharing your experience in a talk or workshop
  • networking with Central Europe's AI talent
  • supporting the meetup in other ways

reach out to the organisers at info@v4air.eu
