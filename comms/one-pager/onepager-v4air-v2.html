Join the V4 AI Researchers Meetup

Co-organized by prg.ai and Charles University

In April 2027, PhD students, researchers and experts implementing AI methods from across Central Europe will meet for three days at V4AIR near Prague. We are inviting our partners and collaborators to join them.

What does a bee researcher and a traffic engineer have in common?

They both use computer vision.
They are both coming to V4AIR.

Our goal is to bring Central Europe's applied AI research and industry into the same room.

  • Kostelec Castle, 30 minutes from Prague
  • 28–30 April 2027
  • Three days, with accommodation at the castle

Come meet researchers applying methods like NLP, computer vision, machine learning, agentic AI and statistical modeling across fields like biology, medicine, linguistics, psychology and engineering.

If you're interested in:

  • meeting someone with specific expertise to your issue
  • sharing your experience in a talk or workshop
  • networking with Central Europe's AI talent
  • supporting the meetup in other ways

Reach out to the organizers at info@v4air.eu
