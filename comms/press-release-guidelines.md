# Press release guidelines

1. Issue the release before the initiative starts.
2. Write the news in the first two sentences. They must stand on their own.
3. Say why the news matters.
4. Use the next one or two paragraphs for the news. Then say who is behind the initiative.
5. Include:
   - why it is happening and the issue behind it
   - who is running it and their qualifications
   - the expected timeline
   - a quote from a senior person in the lead organisation
6. Include only facts.
7. End with contact details: name, phone, email, website.
