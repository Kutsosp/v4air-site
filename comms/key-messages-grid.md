Draft, 28.09.2026. The summary and the key messages are in `key-messages-table.md`. Key message numbers below refer to that file. The grid uses the five columns of the University of Reading "Communications Planning template" (Research Communications, February 2020).

This part covers the audiences reached through the partner universities. Industry experts, managers, founders and investors are covered in the industry part, which prg.ai develops. The general public is covered by the public outreach partners.
