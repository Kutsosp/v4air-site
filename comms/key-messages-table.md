# V4AIR academic communication: key messages

Draft, 28.09.2026.

## Summary

> V4AIR, the V4 AI Researchers Meetup, brings early-career researchers and graduate students from the four Visegrad countries to Kostelec Castle near Prague on 28–30 April 2027. The meetup is for anyone who uses or builds AI methods in their research, in any discipline. Over three days, participants meet the people their research needs, and they can apply with unfinished work. The programme is built from what you say you want to learn, who you want to meet and what interests you. Participation is fully funded for students and researchers from the six partner universities, with support from the International Visegrad Fund. Applications close on 15 January 2027.
